The Trickle-Down Effect

Every deal made at the table opens doors below it — acquisition opportunities through ETA Factory / CAF, contracts that flow to smaller Black- and Brown-owned vendors, capital that recycles into the next member's deal. The room compounds; it doesn't just distribute.

How this connects to NC's political map — 41 Black legislators, a #7 national ranking in Black buying power, and the specific asks that turn both into results — is part of what members work through together inside the room.
